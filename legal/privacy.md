## Introduction

Tweeting Birds LLC ("Tweeting Birds," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Quips app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac, including its widgets, share extension, and Shortcuts actions ("App"), and when you visit our website at quipsapp.com ("Website").

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

When you use Quips, you may provide the following information. It is stored in the App on your device (and in your iCloud account if iCloud is on for Quips); we do not receive it:

- **Quotes and Content:** Text content of quotes, notes, and sources you create, including text you share into the App from other apps or paste from your clipboard
- **Authors:** Names and information about quote authors you add, such as a short bio and dates
- **Tags and Collections:** Tags and collections you create to organize your quotes, and the Public Collections you add
- **App Settings:** Your preferences for app appearance, themes, colors, Share Studio styles, widgets, and Daily Quote schedules

The App reads your clipboard only when you ask it to, such as when you choose to create a new quote from the clipboard.

### Information You Send Us

- **Support and Feedback Emails:** If you contact us — including from the App's Support or Feedback option, which opens a pre-filled email in your mail app — we receive your email address, your message, and anything you attach. The App pre-fills the email with your App version, operating-system version, device model, and device region to help us troubleshoot; you can see and remove these details before you send. We use this only to respond to you and to fix problems you report.

### Automatically Collected Information

The App does not automatically collect information that identifies you. The following technical information is processed:

- **iCloud Data:** If iCloud is turned on for Quips in your device settings, your quote data is synced to your personal iCloud account (managed by Apple, not us)
- **Purchase Information:** If you make in-app purchases, transaction data is processed by Apple through the App Store. The App checks your subscription status with Apple on your device; we do not receive your name, Apple Account, or payment information
- **Crash and Performance Diagnostics:** If the App crashes, freezes, or runs slowly, it sends a diagnostic report to Sentry — see [Crash and Performance Reporting](#crash-reporting) below
- **Requests for Public Content:** When the App downloads public content such as Collections, announcements, FAQs, or these legal documents, our hosting providers receive ordinary request information — see [Data the App Requests From Our Website](#app-requests) below

This is the complete list. Nothing else leaves your device without you asking it to.

### Information We Do NOT Collect (In the App)

Within the Quips app itself, we want to be clear about what we don't collect:

- The App does not collect your name, email address, or contact information (we only see these if you email us)
- The App does not track your location, and it never uses your location, camera, microphone, or photo library in the background. A feature that needs one of them uses it only when you choose that feature, and only after your device asks your permission. Anything it captures is kept with that quote in your library, on your device (and in your iCloud account if iCloud is on for Quips), and we never receive it
- The App does not access your contacts
- The App does not collect device identifiers for tracking purposes
- The App does not use advertising networks, ad tracking, or an advertising identifier
- The App does not use product-analytics services that record what you tap, read, or save
- We do not sell your data to third parties

The App does record some of its own usage events — which card style you shared with, how many quotes you imported — but these are written only to the device's system log, where they are readable in a developer tool attached to your own Mac. They are never uploaded, and we never see them. The same is true of the launch-time, responsiveness, and memory measurements the App reads from Apple's on-device MetricKit service: they are written to the device's system log only.

Crash and performance diagnostics are the one thing the App does send, and they are described in full in [Crash and Performance Reporting](#crash-reporting). Separately, if you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with aggregated crash and usage statistics under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/); this does not identify you to us.

The optional Daily Quote feature uses local notifications that are scheduled entirely on your device. If you turn it on, the App picks a quote from your own library and asks your device's operating system to show it at your chosen time — no data leaves your device, and we never know whether you use the feature.

Widgets, the share extension, Spotlight search, and Shortcuts actions read your quotes on your device. If you use Siri or Shortcuts with Quips, Apple processes those requests under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/); we do not receive them.

The App's suggestions — suggested tags, filling in a quote's author and source from text you paste or share, Share Studio styles, related quotes, search by meaning, and Tidy Library — are made on your device by the models built into Apple's operating system. Your quotes are not sent to us or to anyone else to make them, and we never see what was suggested or what you accepted. Share Studio illustrations are created in Image Playground, Apple's own system feature; the App opens it with a short scene description suggested on your device (which you can change) rather than the text of your quote, turns off personalization from your photo library, and what Image Playground does with it is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Searching Discover also happens on your device, against a search index the App downloads; we do not receive what you search for.

Our website is different: if you choose to sign up for our newsletter or free email course, we do collect the email address (and optionally the name) you provide, in order to send you those emails, and the website uses cookieless aggregate analytics. See [Website](#website-newsletter) below for details.

## How We Use Your Information {#how-we-use-your-information}

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the App's functionality:

1. **Local Storage:** All quotes, authors, and tags are stored locally on your device
2. **iCloud Sync:** Your data is synced across your devices through your personal iCloud account
3. **App Functionality:** Your settings and preferences are used to customize your experience
4. **Purchase Management:** Subscription status is managed through Apple's StoreKit to provide premium features
5. **Fixing Problems:** Crash and performance diagnostics are used only to find, understand, and fix bugs and slowdowns
6. **Support:** Emails you send us are used only to answer you and resolve what you reported
7. **Website Emails:** Newsletter and email-course signups are used only to send you the emails you asked for

We do not use any of this information for advertising, to build a profile of you, or to make automated decisions that affect you.

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage

- All your quotes and data are stored locally on your device
- We cannot access your device data
- Data persists on your device even when offline

### iCloud Storage

- Your data is stored in the private database of your personal iCloud account
- We use Apple's CloudKit framework, which Apple encrypts in transit and at rest (and end-to-end if you turn on Apple's Advanced Data Protection)
- We cannot access your iCloud data

### Security Measures

We rely on industry-standard security measures:

- Data encryption using Apple's built-in security features, including Data Protection for the App's files on iPhone and iPad
- Encrypted (HTTPS) connections for everything the App downloads, and for in-app purchases
- No sharing of your quotes or other content with third parties
- Regular security updates

No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security — but because your content never reaches our servers, there is very little of it for us to protect or lose.

## Your Rights and Choices {#your-rights-and-choices}

You have complete control over your data:

### Access and Control

- **View Your Data:** All your quotes are visible within the App
- **Edit Your Data:** Modify or update quotes, authors, and tags at any time
- **Delete Your Data:** Delete individual quotes (they stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, then are removed), or delete every quote, author, and tag at once from Settings > Import & Export. With iCloud on, deletions sync to your other devices and your iCloud account
- **Export Your Data:** Export your library as a file from Settings > Import & Export, to back it up or move it elsewhere

Because we never receive your quotes, we can't view, export, or delete them for you — these controls are in your hands.

### iCloud Sync Control

- Turn iCloud sync for Quips on or off at any time in your device's iCloud settings (on iPhone and iPad: Settings > [Your Name] > iCloud; on Mac: System Settings > [Your Name] > iCloud)
- Deleting the App will not delete your iCloud data; you must manage iCloud data separately, for example through your device's iCloud storage settings

### Subscription Management

- Manage your subscription through the App Store
- View, change, or cancel your subscription with Manage Subscription in the App, or in your Apple Account's subscription settings
- Subscription data is managed by Apple; we do not store payment information

If you sign up for the Quote Unquote newsletter or the free 7-day email course on our website, we collect the information you provide in that form — typically your email address, and optionally your first name. This information is sent to and processed by **MailerLite**, our email delivery provider, who sends the emails on our behalf and acts as our data processor for this purpose. When you submit a form, MailerLite also records standard signup details such as the time and your IP address, and may ask you to confirm your subscription by email. For the emails we send, MailerLite records delivery, bounces, unsubscribes, and which links are clicked; we have turned off open tracking. We use these statistics only in aggregate, to see whether the emails are useful.

Nothing is sent to MailerLite unless you actually submit a signup form. Our pages do not load MailerLite scripts or tracking pixels — simply visiting a page with a signup form on it sends nothing to MailerLite.

### Cookies and Local Storage

Our website does not set cookies. If you choose a light or dark theme, your browser remembers that choice in its local storage on your device; it is never sent to us.

### Web Analytics

Our website uses **Cloudflare Web Analytics** to count page views so we can see which pages people find useful. It is a privacy-first analytics service: it sets no cookies, does not fingerprint your browser for advertising, does not track you across other sites, and gives us only aggregate numbers (like total visits per page). It cannot identify you as an individual, and we couldn't use it to do so even if we wanted to. See [Cloudflare's Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for details.

### Data the App Requests From Our Website {#app-requests}

The Quips app fetches public content — such as curated Collections and the Discover search index, in-app announcements, FAQs, and the text of these legal documents — from quipsapp.com and data.quipsapp.com. These requests are served by GitHub Pages and Cloudflare's content delivery network. Like any web request, they generate standard server request metadata (such as IP address, timestamp, and the file requested), which GitHub and Cloudflare log as part of operating their infrastructure. These requests carry nothing from your library. We do not access, use, or combine this metadata to identify or track individual users.

If you open a link from an in-app announcement, the web page loads inside the App, and that website's own privacy policy applies to it.

### Crash and Performance Reporting {#crash-reporting}

The App uses **Sentry** to report crashes and performance problems, so we can find and fix them. This is diagnostics, not analytics: it tells us that something broke, not what you were reading or saving when it did.

What a report contains: the type of crash or slowdown, where in our code it happened, timing measurements, a short trail of recent technical events leading up to it (such as which screen was open or which of our own servers the App was contacting), technical device details (such as device model, available memory and storage, language and region settings, and the operating-system and app versions), and a random identifier that Sentry's software generates for this installation of the App so that repeated reports of the same problem can be grouped. What it never contains: your quotes, authors, tags, notes, collections, the text you type, your name, or your email address. We configure the Sentry SDK with personally identifying information switched off, so it does not attach your IP address to a report.

Reports are not linked to your identity, and are never used to track you or to build a profile of you. Performance reports are sampled — most sessions send nothing at all. Diagnostics are sent only from release versions of the App — the App Store version and TestFlight betas; builds we run during development report nothing. Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the App stable and working. The App does not currently have a setting to turn these reports off; because they contain none of your content and are not linked to you, we consider this a fair balance, but you may contact us with any objection.

This is what the App's privacy label on the App Store declares as Crash Data and Performance Data, and it is the only information the App reports about your use of it. (Downloading public content — Collections, announcements, these legal documents — also produces ordinary server request logs at our hosting providers, described under [Website](#website-newsletter) above.) See [Sentry's Privacy Policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/) for details.
